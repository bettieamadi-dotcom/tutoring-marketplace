Need some answers?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about learning and teaching with Tutor Pearl.

What is Tutor Pearl?

Tutor Pearl is an online tutoring platform that connects students with tutors for virtual lessons across different subjects and skills.

How do I register as a student?

Select Start as a Student or Register as a Student, complete the registration form and create your account.

How can I become a tutor?

Select Teach With Us or Register as a Tutor and complete the tutor application. Your application will be reviewed.

Are lessons conducted online?

Yes. Tutor Pearl lessons are virtual. Students and tutors connect online for their scheduled lessons.

Do tutors need a university degree?

A bachelor's degree is not a general requirement. Tutors should have good knowledge of the subjects they teach and be able to explain concepts clearly, communicate patiently and support learners.

Can I use a phone or a computer?

Yes. A suitable smartphone, tablet or computer can be used, provided you have a reliable internet connection and the equipment needed for your lessons.

How do students book lessons?

Students can explore available tutors, review their profiles and submit a booking request for a suitable lesson time.

How do I contact Tutor Pearl?

You can contact our support team by email at tutorpearlsupport@gmail.com for assistance.
