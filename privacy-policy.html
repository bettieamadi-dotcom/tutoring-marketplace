Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to Tutor Pearl. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information of students, tutors and visitors who use our platform.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices and rights available to you.

By using Tutor Pearl, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

Effective date: 4 November 2026

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Tutor Pearl, we may collect:

  • Account information: Your name, email address, account identification and authentication information.
  • Profile information: Tutor qualifications, subjects, experience, profile descriptions and other details you choose to provide.
  • Booking information: Lesson dates, times, duration, subject, booking status and messages submitted with bookings.
  • Payment information: Transaction references, payment status, amounts, currency and related payment records. Payment card or bank credentials are handled by the relevant payment provider where applicable.
  • Technical information: Information such as browser type, device information, IP address and security records where made available through our hosting and service providers.
  • Google Calendar information: When a tutor connects Google Calendar, we receive the permissions needed to create lesson calendar events and Google Meet links.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Create and manage student and tutor accounts.
  • Display tutor profiles and facilitate tutoring services.
  • Manage lesson bookings and communications.
  • Process and record payments and platform commissions.
  • Provide customer support and respond to enquiries.
  • Maintain platform security and prevent misuse.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.
  • Improve the operation and reliability of Tutor Pearl.

4. Google Calendar and Google Meet

Tutors may voluntarily connect their Google accounts to Tutor Pearl to enable calendar event creation and Google Meet functionality for lessons.

When a tutor authorizes this connection, Tutor Pearl requests access to the Google Calendar events permission (calendar.events).

This permission is used to:

  • Create calendar events for eligible lessons.
  • Generate Google Meet links for lessons.
  • Associate meeting details with the relevant booking.
  • Help tutors and students access their scheduled lessons.

Tutor Pearl does not use Google Calendar data for advertising, sell it, or use it for unrelated purposes.

Google OAuth refresh tokens used to maintain an authorized calendar connection are encrypted before storage. Access tokens are used to perform authorized calendar operations.

Calendar event information and meeting links may be stored in Tutor Pearl's database so that the relevant tutor and student can access their lesson details.

Tutors can disconnect Google Calendar through the connection controls in their Tutor Pearl dashboard. Disconnecting stops future calendar access through that connection. Existing calendar events may need to be managed separately in Google Calendar.

Google's use of information is also governed by Google's own privacy policy and applicable Google API policies.

5. Payments and Financial Records

Tutor Pearl may store payment transaction records necessary to manage bookings, confirm successful payments, calculate platform commissions and maintain earnings records.

Payments may be handled by third-party payment providers. Their handling of payment information is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information to third parties. We may share relevant information only where necessary:

  • With tutors and students to facilitate bookings and lessons.
  • With Google when a tutor authorizes calendar and meeting functionality.
  • With hosting, database, security and infrastructure providers that help operate Tutor Pearl.
  • With payment providers to process or verify transactions.
  • Where required by law or necessary to protect the rights, safety and security of users and the platform.

Service providers may process information in countries outside Nigeria. Where applicable, we take steps to ensure that such processing is subject to appropriate safeguards.

7. Data Storage and Security

We use service providers and reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or loss.

These measures include access controls and security protections appropriate to the information being handled. Sensitive Google refresh tokens are encrypted before being stored.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their account credentials and notify us if they suspect unauthorized access.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including account management, bookings, payment records, security, dispute resolution and legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we take appropriate steps to delete it or otherwise securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate retention needs.
